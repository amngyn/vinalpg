    VINA LPG CO., LTD

    About Us

    VINA LPG specializes in consulting, supplying, and supervising the installation of equipment and machinery for LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) and NG (Natural Gas) industries for gas projects including: storage facilities, LPG tankers, LPG filling stations, NG substations for power plants and industrial facilities, etc...

    In collaboration with experts from the USA, Italy, Germany, and Singapore, Vina LPG also provides consulting, design, installation, and commissioning services for projects:

    • Installation of spherical tanks up to 2,500 tons
    • Construction of LPG filling plants with automatic and semi-automatic conveyor systems
    • Creation and installation of gas pressure reducing stations, gas treatment; metering, control, and natural gas distribution systems
